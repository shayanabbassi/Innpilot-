## Not covered or carried forward

- **SEC-003, signed URLs.** There are no uploaded files yet (D12: attachments are references). Expiring download
  links come with file storage in Phase 6.
- **Browser checks with data.** The demo hotel has no open guest balances, so the guest balances screen with data
  and the dashboard's balances panel are covered by `aging.e2e` and `dashboard.e2e` only; the front desk view of the
  dashboard is covered by `dashboard.e2e` only. Excel files were checked as valid workbooks by the tests and the
  download was triggered from the screen; they were not opened in Excel itself.
- **Performance.** One user at a time on a development machine; load under many users is part of Phase 6. Exports
  above 50,000 rows are refused (EXPORT_TOO_LARGE) until background exports arrive with the job runner (Phase 6).
- **Known limits.** Receivables are grouped by guest; grouping by company needs company billing accounts, not
  built. Purchase tax has no tax code, so recoverable purchase tax is one figure. Room revenue is read by posting
  date, so a night billed at check-out without a nightly accrual shows on the check-out date. Operators have no
  self-service password reset: a new operator account is created with the CLI.
- **Accountant to confirm.** The report group of each account (current or non-current), the P&L layout and the
  direct cash-flow classification.
- **From earlier phases, still open.** The on-account check-out screen is covered by the API test only (Phase 3).
- **Environment.** Docker Desktop stopped on its own during earlier phases; during this phase the suite ran without
  interruption.
